This *happy little accident* has been quite fun to build, and I've started to enjoy working with it.

I've been able to establish a nice design philosophy for how I'll shape this project: functional unless absolutely necessary. No OOP with its stupid `this` chains (which I *always* forget to type), and *absolutely* **no build steps!**

One thing I'm not a huge fan of is the name *"ObservableValue"* for the core reactive primitive (and *only* class) of this otherwise functional-style library, so once again I'm conjuring inspiration from Norse mythology which is something I've always had a penchant for.

Hmm... Reactive graphs.

Causality... *Fate*... Maybe *"wyrd"*?

No, that would imply inevitability, and this is definitely just deterministic computation.

The seamless binding of reactive properties in vanilla TypeScript *has* felt like *magic* at times, though.

Wait, *magic..?*

Oh, *yeah!* The Old Norse word *"seiðr"* translates to *magic of influence and causality*.

*"Seidr"* has a really nice ring to it. *So be it!*

**Rise, my creation!** I name you... *[Seidr](https://github.com/fimbul-works/seidr#readme)!*
